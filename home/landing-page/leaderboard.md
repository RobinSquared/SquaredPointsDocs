# Leaderboard

## Leaderboard

Season rankings update as eligible points land. Each season gives the community a new race to join.

### Your season score

Your season score tracks points earned during the current season. It helps determine your place on the leaderboard.

### Your lifetime level

Your lifetime level reflects your long-term progress. Redeeming a perk never reduces it.

{% hint style="info" %}
Spend available points without losing your lifetime level or season score.
{% endhint %}
