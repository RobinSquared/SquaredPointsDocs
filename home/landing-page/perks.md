# Perks

## Perks

Use your available Squared Points to redeem expressive community perks. Redemptions do not reduce your lifetime level or season score.

### Available perks

* **Profile accent colour** — 100 SP
* **Square Splash alert** — 100 SP
* **Leaderboard spotlight** — 200 SP
* **Geometric title** — 250 SP

Perks use your available balance only.
