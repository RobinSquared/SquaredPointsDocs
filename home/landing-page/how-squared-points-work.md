# How Squared Points work

## How Squared Points work

Squared Points gives the RobinSquared community one shared score. Earn points through eligible stream games and Discord activity.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Join your way

Your first eligible catch, stream interaction, or Discord activity creates a private Squared Points profile.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Earn across the community

LurkBait catches and eligible Discord activity build the same Squared Points account.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Redeem perks

Perks use your available balance. Your lifetime level and season score never decrease.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Eligible activity is added to your account as points land.
{% endhint %}
