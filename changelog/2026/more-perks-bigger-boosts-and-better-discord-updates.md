# More perks, bigger boosts & better Discord updates

This update brings more ways to reward your progress, new profile touches, better Discord announcements, and safer maintenance and recovery. It does not reset your SP, levels, purchase history or existing season scores.

### Boosters up to 10×

* Added support for personal, community and scheduled boosters up to **10× eligible base SP**
* Improved multiplier labels and explanations when a boost is limited by the community's settings.
* Booster strength still depends on eligible activities, duration, usage limits and applicable bonus caps. This update does not automatically increase existing boosters or earning rates.
* Personal and community bonuses add together rather than multiplying each other. You receive the stronger of your tier bonus or applicable boosters, not both stacked together.

### New rewards to claim

Two optional rewards are available through **Your account → Reward claims**:

* **Season welcome:** a starting reward of 25 SP, claimable once during each active main season.
* **Connected accounts:** a starting reward of 50 SP, claimable once after linking your Twitch and Discord accounts. Already linked? You can qualify too.

These require a manual claim and acceptance of the current Terms. Claiming does not make your profile public, and boosters do not multiply these rewards.

### More benefits for your tier

* Added earning bonuses, starting at up to **10%**, and higher ordinary chat/voice allowances, starting at up to **25%**.
* Added discounts on selected store items, starting at up to **10%**, plus support for tier-required rewards.
* Added a private **Tier benefits** view so you can see the earning perks and allowances available to you.

Your level thresholds are unchanged. Spending SP still does not lower your level or leaderboard score.

### Make your profile yours

* Added six geometric tier frames, with access to frames at or below your earned tier.
* Added featured achievements: choose your favourites and arrange their order, with starting allowances of **one to six slots** across the tiers.
* Find these options in **Your account → Frames & highlights**. Your full achievement collection remains intact.

Changing your appearance does not publish a private profile or remove purchased cosmetics from your inventory.

### More flexible seasons

* Seasons can now have updated names, descriptions and future end dates without rewriting existing scores or their original start date.
* Added scheduled main seasons and clearer season-break displays. A new season no longer has to begin immediately after the previous one.
* During ordinary season breaks, you can keep earning spendable SP and lifetime progress, without adding to a main-season leaderboard.
* The current champion keeps their recognition until the next main season closes.

### A fresh look for Discord

* Announcements now support compact violet-accented cards, clear event headings and buttons to relevant public pages such as the Store, Goals, Polls and Leaderboards.
* Added role notifications for selected announcement types.
* Role notifications have a 15-minute cooldown for each announcement type in each channel. Cards can still appear during the cooldown without another role ping. Your Discord notification settings still apply.
* Added rotating bot activities that can show public season information, open goals, open polls and shared boosters.
* The bot can show pause and planned-shutdown notices instead of its normal activity while connected. Normal rotation returns after resuming or cancelling shutdown.

### Clearer purchases, policies and privacy

* New purchases retain the agreed item details and price while awaiting approval or delivery. Later store edits do not change that purchase's reserved price or refund amount.
* Improved retry protection for purchases and Twitch Extension actions.
* Added screens for published Terms, Privacy notices and previous versions. You may be asked to accept current Terms before purchasing, voting, contributing or linking accounts.
* Automatic earnings, balance/history access, export, deletion and opting out remain available without accepting new Terms.
* Added more specific staff permissions. Access to a member's full private transaction history requires explicit permission and a recorded reason.

### Safer maintenance and recovery

Planned shutdowns now have clearer website and overlay warnings. Squared Points stops accepting new transactions during shutdown and lets accepted work finish. Recorded whole-service outages can extend supported purchased booster timers, cosmetic rentals and pending delivery deadlines; they do not create missed engagement SP.

A new guided backup-recovery process adds checks, a protected rollback copy and owner review before returning to normal service. Restored sessions are signed out, the service returns paused, and old announcements or uncertain rewards are held back instead of blindly replayed.

**Recovery is not a rewind button for everything.** Restoring an older backup can lose newer local progress and cannot undo actions already delivered on Twitch, Discord or OBS. The current workflow supports compatible backups on the same installation, not every disaster-recovery scenario. Automatic earning resumes only after owner review/unpause and the recovery screen's stated Sydney-day boundary.

### Still to come

Vanity URLs, profile uploads, quests, bounties and special-competition prizes are not included in this update. Unfinished features remain unavailable while work continues.
